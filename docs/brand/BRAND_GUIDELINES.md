# RoyaltyRails Brand Guidelines

## Brand position

RoyaltyRails should communicate **premium financial infrastructure for creators and rights holders**: precise, trustworthy, automated, global, and built around payment flow.

## Logo system

Use the full primary lockup when space permits. Use the monogram/icon for compact navigation, badges, favicons, and small UI surfaces. Use the wordmark where horizontal space is available but the full lockup would be too tall.

### Clear space

Maintain clear space around the logo. As a practical minimum, keep surrounding content at least **10% of the logo width** away from the mark.

### Minimum size

- Full logo: avoid rendering below **240 px** wide in digital interfaces.
- Wordmark: avoid rendering below **160 px** wide.
- Icon: use a dedicated simplified icon at small sizes.

## Approved palette

- Primary Red — `#DD1313`
- Royal Gold — `#D4AF37`
- Accent Orange — `#F08A24`
- Background Black — `#000000`
- Surface Charcoal — `#0D0D0D`
- Primary Light — `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary Muted — `#B8B8B8`

Recommended balance: red leads, gold signals royalty/value, orange is a restrained motion/highlight accent. Black and charcoal provide the premium base.

## Typography

- **Playfair Display** — display headings, brand moments, editorial emphasis.
- **Inter** — UI, navigation, labels, body copy, and product surfaces.

## Do

- Use the approved assets and palette.
- Preserve proportions and clear space.
- Favor dark backgrounds for flagship brand presentations.
- Maintain high contrast and legibility.
- Keep orange as a restrained accent rather than a dominant color.

## Don't

- Stretch, skew, rotate, or distort the logo.
- Recolor it with unapproved palettes.
- Add unrelated crowns, shadows, outlines, glows, or effects to the canonical identity.
- Overcrowd the mark with copy or UI elements.
- Auto-trace raster masters and label the result the official production vector master.

## Repository display assets

The SVG files under `assets/` are optimized display derivatives for GitHub and documentation. High-resolution raster masters remain the approved source artwork until a dedicated production vector master is commissioned.
